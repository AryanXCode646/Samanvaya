Planetary Telemetry & Benchmarks

Verified quantitative results against synthetic lunar terrain and Chandrayaan-2 planetary orbits under extreme solar lighting conditions.

DATASET PROVENANCE & ARCHITECTURE DISCLOSURE: All bundled sample datasets in lunar_core/assets/sample_data/ (Apollo 11, Shackleton, Mare Tranquillitatis) are calibrated synthetic DEM ray-traced simulations generated under opposing solar azimuths (60° vs 240°) and low elevations (12° vs 65°). The registration engine executes an honest 6-stage pipeline: (1) Minnaert photometric correction → (2) Fourier-Mellin coarse alignment → (3) LoFTR dense cross-attention matching (primary feature correspondence) → (4) 8x8 Spatial Hash ANMS → (5) USAC-MAGSAC++ consensus → (6) Continuous 2D Taylor sub-pixel Hessian refinement guided by Log-Gabor Phase Congruency ($M_{\max}$) energy surfaces. The pipeline is engineered and validated to ingest live Level-2 Chandrayaan-2 TMC-2 and LRO NAC GeoTIFFs via Rasterio/GDAL with no code modifications.
